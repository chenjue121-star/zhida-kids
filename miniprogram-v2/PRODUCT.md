# Zhida Phase A + B + C + D

## Register
Product: native WeChat family and child management for caregivers using a phone during everyday family routines.

## Principles
White surfaces, restrained accessible green actions, fixed type sizes, native controls, explicit permissions, honest empty/error states. Recommendations use real owned garments; no fabricated garments or child photo uploads. Explicit preferences remain distinct from feedback-derived inferred tags. Garment photos follow the authorized B intake flow. Preserve edits on failures and keep every account/family isolated.

## Phase B
Family wardrobe represents real physical garments; fit is per child and availability is shared. Continuous capture and album selection upload private garment photos into owner-only resumable drafts. Recognition suggests attributes; explicit confirmation creates garments. Empty model configuration offers manual entry. Unknown operation results are queried before retry, permission changes invalidate drafts, and archived garments retain history. Keep the approved white/green layout, centered independent dropdown arrows, round selection markers, and 44px controls. The four primary destinations are outfit, wardrobe, calendar, and account.

## Phase C
Daily suggestions resolve today or tomorrow to an actual date in the family timezone and use that date's forecast. Fit, availability, comfort and coordinated combinations apply equally to provider ranking and rule fallback. Unknown weather requires a caregiver's explicit check before selection. Suggestions and local drafts reserve nothing. Fixed items and temporary exclusions belong only to their draft; replacements, backups and sharing periods require confirmation.

Selection stores an immutable snapshot and reserves its main and confirmed backup garments. Multiple plans keep independent sequence numbers; editing preserves a plan's identity and increments its version. Sharing binds all current source plans and nonoverlapping periods. Packing counts physical garment IDs once. An unknown save retains its original operation and payload; other drafts can be prepared, while same-family/date selection waits for recovery. Original success is shown as its historical receipt, with current changes separately explained. Permission and navigation changes reject late responses. Calendar, virtual try-on and preference learning remain outside this phase.

## Phase D
Calendar reads selected plan snapshots by actual date in the family timezone, supports same-day multi-plan history and cancelled filters, and never describes elapsed plans as worn. Cancellation increments the source version, retains its snapshot and releases current garment reservations. Unknown cancellation keeps the original operation and frozen payload; only its authoritative receipt or NOT_EXECUTED result resolves uncertainty.

Historical reuse stages a new target-date draft, including new weather, current child fit and garment availability, preferences and occupancy checks. Updated garment details require explicit acknowledgement. Source snapshots remain unchanged, prior sharing grants and draft constraints are cleared, and final selection uses the Phase C review/save flow. Selected and cancelled historical sources can both prepare new drafts. No draft creates reservations.

Feedback has an explicit plan/version and stable event identity. Family-basis feedback belongs to family preferences; member-basis feedback belongs to the acting member's own preferences. Selected plans are weaker evidence than explicit aesthetic feedback. Comfort, washing, missing garments, size and skipped reasons do not become aesthetic dislike. Inferred tags show their evidence separately from explicit selections and can be accepted, edited through explicit preferences, or dismissed. Viewers and other members' profiles remain read-only. Refresh versions on return and reject late reads, photos and writes after account, family, permission or navigation changes.
